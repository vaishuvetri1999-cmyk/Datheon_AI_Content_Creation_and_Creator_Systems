# GUCCI

**GUCCI – SOCIAL MEDIA CONTENT STRATEGY**

**Project Overview**

This project presents a 30-day social media content strategy inspired by Gucci's luxury fashion identity. It includes brand research, brand voice guidelines, AI-powered content creation prompts, 50 engaging content hooks, a monthly publishing calendar, and a performance tracking framework.

**Objective:** To develop creative, engaging, and consistent social media content while maintaining a premium luxury-fashion identity.

**Project Type:** Independent student marketing project.
